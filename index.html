COMPETITION RULES OVERVIEW

Please review the official regulations. Key points below protect the integrity of the blind judging process.

Equipment & Prep

  • • Allowed fuel: Hardwood, lump charcoal, and wood pellets are permitted. Electric/gas is allowed for **ignition only**. No gas-only or electric-only cooking.
  • • BYO Smoker: Teams must bring their own cooking equipment (smokers, grills, or pits). No cooking units will be provided.
  • • Pre-Cooking Rule: All meat must start raw, unmarinated, and unseasoned on-site. Inspection will take place on-site at **06:00 AM**. No pre-cooking or seasoning at home.
  • • BYO Meat: Teams must supply their own competition meat (Pork and Beef Brisket). All meat must be brought to the venue raw and unseasoned.
  • • Station Safety: All stations must have a working fire extinguisher, food-grade prep surfaces, sanitising stations, and meat thermometers.

Box Presentation & Scoring

  • • Zero Garnish Policy: **Strictly no garnish of any kind is permitted in the turn-in box.** (No greens, lettuce, parsley, kale, cilantro/coriander). Just the meat.
  • • No Foreign Objects: Avoid toothpicks, skewers, foil, twine, or markings inside the boxes. Any foreign object counts as an infraction.
  • • Weighted 60-Point Scale: Entries are scored by a panel of judges on **Taste** (30 points), **Texture** (20 points), and **Presentation** (10 points). Tie-breakers follow a count-back system starting with Taste.
  • • Judges' Decisions: **All judges' decisions are final.** Scoring is conducted blindly and independently. No appeals, disputes, or score reviews will be entered into.
